# Known limitations

- No expert Jyotishi review has been completed.
- Jagannatha Hora 8.0 could not be automated: the official Windows installer
  was downloaded and attempted, but no CLI/report export was available in this
  environment.
- Shadbala-family formulas remain experimental pending identical formula and
  configuration mapping to independent references.
- Bhava Chalit, dasha anchoring/eligibility, Upagrahas, Avasthas, regional
  Muhurta tables and extended Vargas vary by tradition.
- Ashtakavarga Shodhya Pinda references contain a printed-book versus
  calculator discrepancy.
- PyJHora is development-only validation evidence and is not a runtime
  dependency of the package.
- Swiss Ephemeris free AGPL usage requires compliance with AGPL-3.0-or-later;
  proprietary distribution requires separate legal review and potentially the
  Swiss Ephemeris Professional Licence.
