# Integration Known Limitations

- Jagannatha Hora 8.0 parity validation is pending. The official package was
  attempted, but no reproducible automated report/export comparison was
  available in the execution environment.
- Jyotishi expert review is pending for the engine's convention-sensitive
  families. Technical tests do not constitute expert certification.
- Divisional-chart alternatives outside
  `parashara_bphs_chapter_6_v1`, especially D5/D6/D8/D11 alternatives, remain
  experimental and are not default-enabled.
- Shadbala, Bhava Bala, Ishta/Kashta, Vimsopaka, some Ashtakavarga operations,
  special Lagnas, Upagrahas, and Avasthas retain provisional or experimental
  status.
- Missing external fixtures remain for complete JHora parity and for some
  published strength and convention tables with identical settings.
- Polar or unavailable rise/set events are represented as null and require
  consumer handling. Regional Muhurta tables may differ and are configurable.
- Birth-time sensitivity is a deterministic boundary-analysis tool, not
  rectification or a claim of birth-time certainty.
- Experimental, provisional, or validation-pending outputs must not be
  marketed as validated predictions, expert conclusions, or production-
  certified astrology.
